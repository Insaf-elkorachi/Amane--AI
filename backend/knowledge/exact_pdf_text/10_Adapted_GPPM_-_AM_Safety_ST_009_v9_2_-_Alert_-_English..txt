1.
Purpose:
1.1. The purpose of the procedure is to notify senior management in ArcelorMittal about
significant incidences as soon as they occur.
2.
Scope:
2.1. The procedure applies:

To all ArcelorMittal sites worldwide,

To work related incidences which have resulted in  one or more fatalities or where one
or more people are suffering life threatening injuries.
2.2. Involving:

Any ArcelorMittal employees,

All other persons present in an ArcelorMittal plant (service p roviders, customers,
visitors…)
2.3. The procedure complements the current site and business-related procedures.
3.
Notification 
3.1. The CEO of the business is responsible for ensuring that the procedure is followed. 

Email Notification 
è as soon as possible, at latest 4 hours after the event 

AM H&S Alert:
è max 24 hours after the event

“Closed loop” report to full hierarchy up to CEO office:
è maximum 4 weeks after the event 
“Closed loop” report = full detailed report with root causes analysis, actions plan with 
deadlines and persons in charge, and learning points)
3.2.The notification documents must be transmitted mandatory by E-mail to:
alert@arcelormittal.com
3.3.In addition, the information must also be sent (by the CEO or his/her nominee) to all relevant
Business Unit management and regional company Officers and Directors.
3.4.The Business Unit Health and Safety professional is responsible for sending out to the 
regional H&S organization. Where appropriate, they will also be responsible for ensuring 
that information is disseminated in the regional language.
4.
Accountabilities:
4.1. The CEO is responsible for ensuring adherence to this procedure.
4.2. The Business  Unit  Health and Safety professional is responsible for keeping the  B usiness
Unit procedures updated with any changes to this procedure.
4.3. Corporate Health and Safety are accountable for ensuring the procedure is updated to
account for any changes that may occur from time to time and for reviewing the procedure
on a regular basis.

5. Distribution list 
Alert notification has to be sent to alert@arcelormittal.com (cf. above). 
Corporate H&S will automatically forward to the distribution  defined in document
AM_Safety_ST009_Alert_Appendix1_Distribution List.
6. Notification templates:
6.1. Email Notification: as soon as possible (at latest 0-4 hours after the event) 
The e-mail must be written in English and must include the following elements:

Date:   

Time: 

Personnel: (own / contractor)
Short description: (max 5 lines)
Photographs are not requested at this 
stage.

Contact person: (mandatory)

Name:
Email:

Phone:

Mobile:
6.2. AM Health & Safety Alert: max 24 hours after the event
The email would be followed up on within 24 hours and a Health and Safety Alert would be
issued using the form attached.
The Alert is to include a picture or drawing of the incident to allow for better understanding,
(the pictures or drawings must be downsized to a reasonable volume (< 500 KB) in order to
facilitate downloading by devices such as Qtek, Blackberry
The alert document is published by H&S Corporate in the REX & Alert database

7.3. Presentation to full hierarchy up to CEO office: maximum 4 weeks after the event 
A full root cause analysis will be carried out for these incidences to form the basis of the
presentation to senior management. All fatalities will be discussed with the full hierarchy up
to CEO office. 
Other serious incidences will be presented to the appropriate G roup  M anagement
Committee member and the Regional H&S person.  
The PowerPoint format attached will be used for the presentations. One slide on the
‘Remaining Legal Risks’ (for company and persons) must be added to each presentation.
